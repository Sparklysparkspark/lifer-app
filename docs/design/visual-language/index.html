Lifer visual language: three options

Static mockups for sign-off (V-01). Same sage, cream and charcoal base in all three; they differ in type, taxa colours and illustration. Nothing here is implemented in the app.

Option A: Field guide

Warm book serif for titles, a friendly humanist sans for everything else. Illustrations are flat, solid shapes with cream cut-outs, like a printed field guide.

Type

Life list: 412 species

Caption and footnote text: 12 to 13 px, muted ink.

Group tile illustrations

Chart styling: birds and insects per month

Series use the taxa colours, in a fixed order (birds, mammals, insects, fish), thin gridlines, no chart borders, direct labels where they fit.

Map: quiet basemap, clusters by group

Land and water are two close greys with a warm cast (cream and grey in light, charcoal in dark), borders hairline, no labels below zoom 5, no road colours. Cluster fill is the group with the most photos in it, with the count in the surface colour.
