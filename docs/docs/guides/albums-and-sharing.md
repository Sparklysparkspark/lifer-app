# Albums and sharing

An album is a named group of photos you pick, like "Best raptor shots" or "Portfolio". A photo can be in any number of albums. Removing it from an album, or deleting the album, never deletes the photo.

Open **Albums & trips** in the top bar, on the **Albums** tab.

## Create an album

1. Click **New album**.
2. Enter a **Name** and click **Create**.

Lifer opens the Gallery in select mode. Select photos, then use **Add to album** in the toolbar and choose your new album.

You can also create an album on the spot: every **Add to album** menu has a **New album…** box.

## Add photos

- **From the Gallery:** a photo's **⋯** menu > **Add to album…**, or [select several](./gallery-and-search.md#select) and use **Add to album**. <kbd>Cmd</kbd>+<kbd>A</kbd> in select mode picks every photo matching your search and filters, so you can fill an album from a search in one go.
- **From a species page:** a photo's **⋯** menu > **Add to album…**.
- **From the album:** click **Edit album**, then **Add photos** to pick from your library (the Gallery opens in select mode; click **Add N to album** when done), or **Import album** to import new photos straight into it.

## Edit an album

On the album page:

- Click the title or **Add a description…** to edit them.
- **Edit album > Edit cover** chooses a **Single photo** or **Quad grid** cover. For a grid, hover a tile and click **Change** or **Crop**.
- For a single-photo cover, use a photo's **⋯** menu > **Set as album cover**, then **Adjust position** to frame it.
- To remove photos, use a photo's **⋯** menu > **Remove from album**, or click **Select**, pick several (<kbd>Shift</kbd>-click or drag for a range) and click **Remove from album**.
- Rename or delete an album from the **⋯** menu on its card.

## Viewing an album

- Switch between **Gallery** (every photo) and **Species view** (one card per species in the album).
- **Sort** offers **Newest first**, **Oldest first**, **Highest rated first** and **Lowest rated first**. Albums don't have a custom order.
- **Filters** has **Top rated**, **Media type** (when the album has videos), **RAW files** and **Date**.
- Each photo's **⋯** menu also has **View species**, **Download original** and **Download RAW**.
- To search inside an album, press <kbd>Cmd</kbd>+<kbd>K</kbd> (<kbd>Ctrl</kbd>+<kbd>K</kbd>) on its page. See [quick search](./gallery-and-search.md#quick-search).

## Share links {#share-links}

On a Lifer server, you can share an album with anyone, even people without a Lifer account. Share links aren't available in the desktop app's own library, since it has no public address to give out.

1. Open the album and click **Share…**. The sharing panel opens under the title.
2. Choose the options:
   - **Password (optional):** leave blank for no password.
   - **Allow visitors to download photos**
   - **Show camera info (lens, settings)**
   - **Link expires:** **Never**, **1 day**, **7 days** or **30 days**.
3. Click **Create share link**.
4. The link appears in the list below, showing whether it has a password, allows downloads, and when it expires. Click **Copy** and send it.

A link always shows the album as it is now, so photos you add later appear too.

### What visitors see

A simple page with the album title, the photos, species names, and camera info if you allowed it. There's no menu and no way into the rest of your library. If there's a password, they enter it once, and it's remembered for 24 hours.

Visitors can click a photo to see it full screen and step through the album.

If you allowed downloads, each photo has a download button (hover over it) and so does the full-screen viewer. Visitors get a high-resolution copy of the photo, not your original file or RAW.

**Location is never included in a shared link**, whatever you choose. GPS and place names stay private.

### Stopping a link

A link with an expiry stops working on its own. Until then, the list shows when it expires. After that, it's marked **Expired**.

To stop a link sooner, or one set to **Never**, click **Revoke** next to it. It stops working immediately and for good.

Revoked and expired links stay in the list, dimmed, so you can see what you shared. Create a new link any time.
