# Albums and sharing

An album is a named group of photos you pick, like "Raptors in flight" or "Portfolio". A [smart album](#smart-albums) picks its own photos from rules you set. A photo can be in any number of albums. Removing it from an album, or deleting the album, never deletes the photo.

Open **Albums** in the sidebar (the top bar in a browser). It shows a row of your albums under **My albums** and a row of your [trips](./trips.md) below, each with how many there are. Scroll a row sideways, or click **See all** for the whole list in a grid, with a search by name and the card size under **View**.

## Create an album

1. Click **New album**. Lifer creates an album called "Untitled Album" and opens it with the name selected.
2. Type a name and press <kbd>Enter</kbd>. To keep "Untitled Album", just click elsewhere; you can rename it any time by clicking the name.
3. Click **Add photos** to pick photos from your library (the Gallery opens in select mode; click **Add N to album** when done), or **Import into album** to import new photos straight into it.

An album stays until you delete it, even if you leave it empty.

You can also create an album on the spot: every **Add to album** menu has a **New album…** box.

## Add photos

- **From the Gallery:** a photo's **⋯** menu > **Add to album**, or [select several](./gallery-and-search.md#select) and use **Add to album**. <kbd>Cmd</kbd>+<kbd>A</kbd> in select mode picks every photo matching your search and filters, so you can fill an album from a search in one go.
- **From a species page:** a photo's **⋯** menu > **Add to album**.
- **From the album:** open the **⋯** menu at the end of the toolbar and click **Edit album**, then **Add photos** to pick from your library (the Gallery opens in select mode; click **Add N to album** when done), or **Import into album** to import new photos straight into it.

## Edit an album

On the album page:

- Click the title or **Add a description…** to edit them.
- **⋯** > **Edit album** shows the cover: choose a **Single photo** or **Quad grid** cover, then hover a grid tile and click **Change** or **Crop**, or hover a single-photo cover and click **Crop**. Until you crop it, each cover photo is framed on the animal automatically. Click **Done editing** when you're finished.
- For a single-photo cover, use a photo's **⋯** menu > **Use as album cover**, then **Adjust position** to frame it. Choose **Album cover ✓** to go back to the automatic cover. The automatic cover is an edited photo before a RAW, so an album whose cover is still a RAW switches to your edit once you add one. A cover you chose stays.
- To remove photos, use a photo's **⋯** menu > **Remove from album**, or click **Select**, pick several (<kbd>Shift</kbd>-click or drag for a range) and click **Remove from album**. The photos stay in your library, and **Undo** puts them back in their old places in the album.
- Rename or delete an album from the **⋯** menu on its card. On the album page, **⋯** > **Delete album** deletes it; the photos stay in your library.

## Smart albums {#smart-albums}

A smart album fills itself. Instead of picking photos, you save a set of [Gallery filters and a search](./gallery-and-search.md), and the album always holds every photo that matches them, including ones you import later or rate higher later. Smart albums show a sparkle icon next to their name on their card and page.

There are three ways to make one:

- **From the Gallery:** search or set some filters, then click **Save as smart album** next to **Filters**. Name it (Lifer suggests a name from the filters) and click **Save**.
- **From Albums:** click **New smart album**. The same filter controls as the Gallery's **Filters** panel appear, with a live **Matches N photos** count. Click **Create smart album**. It opens named after its rules, with the name selected: type a new one and press <kbd>Enter</kbd>, or click elsewhere to keep it.
- **From a suggestion:** see below.

### Suggested smart albums {#suggestions}

Click **New smart album** and it starts with **Suggestions**: smart albums worth making from your own photos, each with a preview and how many photos it would hold. Lifer only suggests: nothing is made until you click **Create** on one. The new album opens with its name selected, ready to rename.

Suggestions can include:

- **Best of** a year, and a trip's **highlights**: your 4 and 5 star photos from it.
- **Lifers**: photos from the day you first photographed each species.
- **Rare and legendary**: species in those [rarity tiers](./collection-and-checklists.md#rarity-tiers).
- **Flight shots**, **Behaviour** and **Environmental**, from each photo's shot type.
- A group you photograph a lot, like **Owls**, and the country most of your photos come from, when you've photographed in more than one.
- **Groups of animals** (two or more in the frame) and **Golden hour**.
- **Needs a rating** and **Missing a region**, to help tidy your library.

A suggestion appears only once it would hold at least 8 photos, and never when you already have an album with the same rules. To change one before making it, click its **⋯** > **Customize**: its rules fill in below, change anything you like, then click **Create smart album**. To stop seeing one, click its **⋯** > **Dismiss**. Below the suggestions, **Or choose your own rules** builds one from scratch.

Suggestions are counted in the background, so they're there the moment the dialog opens. After an import or other changes they catch up within a couple of minutes on their own, and as you change your own rules the last count stays, dimmed, until the new one arrives.

On the album page, a line under the title sums up its rules, like "Photos matching: Birds · Flight shots · 5 stars". Click it, or **⋯** > **Edit rules**, to change them; the album updates at once. A long summary is shortened to one line: point at it to read it all.

You decide what belongs:

- To take out a photo that matches but doesn't fit, use its **⋯** menu > **Exclude from this album**, or click **Select**, pick several and click **Exclude from this album**. It stays out even though it still matches.
- The same line ends with **N excluded** once you've taken any out. Click it to see them: pick the ones to put back (or **Select all**) and click **Put back N photos**.

Photos can't be added to a smart album by hand, so smart albums don't appear in **Add to album** menus. To include more photos, edit its rules. Export, share links and the **Species** view all use the album's current matches.

A smart album's cover and grid tiles are always photos it holds. If you change its rules, exclude a photo, or a photo stops matching, a cover you chose that no longer belongs is replaced by the album's first match. A cover you chose that still matches stays.

## Viewing an album

The toolbar has **View**, **Filters**, **Select**, the share button (on a server) and **⋯** for everything else.

- **View** > **Show** switches between **Photos** (every photo) and **Species** (one card per species in the album, each showing the album's own best photo of it: the highest rated, or the first in the album's order).
- In the Photos view, **View** also has **Sort** (**Newest first**, **Oldest first**, **Highest rated first** and **Lowest rated first**; albums don't have a custom order), the thumbnail **Size**, and **Labels**, **Camera info** and **Ratings** under each photo. These three are the same on every page with photos, and a choice made on one page carries over to the others.
- **Filters** (Photos view) are the same as the Gallery's: **Animals**, **Rating**, **Number of animals**, **Rarity**, **Shot type**, **Region**, **Place name**, **Tag**, **Date**, **Media type** (when the album has videos) and **RAW files**.
- Each photo's **⋯** menu also has **View species**, **Download original** and **Download RAW**, and on the desktop app **Show in Finder**.
- To search inside an album, press <kbd>Cmd</kbd>+<kbd>K</kbd> (<kbd>Ctrl</kbd>+<kbd>K</kbd>) on its page. See [quick search](./gallery-and-search.md#quick-search).

## Export an album

**⋯** > **Export** on the album page (or the **⋯** menu on its card) copies the album's photos into a folder, ready for a photo frame, a USB stick or another app.

- **Include RAW files** and **Include .xmp sidecars** add each photo's RAW and its sidecar next to the edited photo.
- **Folders** puts everything in **One folder** or makes **A folder per species**.
- **Hide locations of sensitive species** removes GPS coordinates and the place name from the copies of [sensitive species](#sensitive-species)' photos, keeping only the province or state and country. It's off by default, since an export is often for your own use, and Lifer remembers your last choice. A caption that names the place is rewritten without it.

Then click **Export** and pick the folder; the copy starts as soon as you choose it. The picker opens where you last exported. On a server you can choose from the folders Lifer is allowed to use (the library roots your admin set up), but not the photo library itself.

Exporting only copies: the photos in your library are never moved or changed. A file already in the folder is never overwritten: one with the same name gets a number added, like `IMG_1234 (2).jpg`. Exporting again into the same folder skips what's already there, so you can rerun it to pick up new photos or finish a stopped export. On the desktop app, **Show in Finder** opens the folder when it's done.

## Share links {#share-links}

On a Lifer server, you can share an album with anyone, even people without a Lifer account. Share links aren't available in the desktop app's own library, since it has no public address to give out: there the toolbar has no share button, and the **⋯** menu links to [how to connect the app to a server](../install/connect-desktop-to-server.md) instead.

1. Open the album and click the share button (a box with an arrow) in the toolbar. The sharing panel opens under the title; click the button again to hide it.
2. Choose the options:
   - **Password (optional):** leave blank for no password.
   - **Allow visitors to download photos**
   - **Show camera info (lens, settings)**
   - **Show exact locations:** each photo's place, province or state and country. [Sensitive species](#sensitive-species) show only their province or state and country.
   - **Link expires:** **Never**, **1 day**, **7 days** or **30 days**.
3. Click **Create share link**.
4. Lifer copies the new link for you, ready to paste and send. It also appears in the list below, showing whether it has a password, allows downloads, and when it expires. Click **Copy** to copy it again.

A link always shows the album as it is now, so photos you add later appear too. For a [smart album](#smart-albums), new photos that match its rules appear on the shared page automatically, and photos you exclude drop off it.

### What visitors see

A simple page with the album title, the photos, species names, and camera info and locations if you allowed them. There's no menu and no way into the rest of your library. If there's a password, they enter it once, and it's remembered for 24 hours.

Visitors can click a photo to see it full screen and step through the album.

If you allowed downloads, each photo has a download button (hover over it) and so does the full-screen viewer. Visitors get a high-resolution copy of the photo, not your original file or RAW.

**GPS coordinates are never included in a shared link**, whatever you choose. Place names appear only when **Show exact locations** is on for that link, and you can turn it on or off for an existing link with the **Exact locations** switch in the list.

### Sensitive species {#sensitive-species}

Some species' locations shouldn't be shared: nesting raptors, owls, threatened species. Lifer treats a species as sensitive when the IUCN lists it as **Vulnerable**, **Endangered** or **Critically Endangered**, or when it's on eBird's sensitive species list.

On share links, a sensitive species' photos show only their province or state and country, even with **Show exact locations** on. Album exports can do the same when you ask them to.

- **Settings** > **Metadata** > **Hide locations of sensitive species** turns this off altogether. It's on by default.
- **Settings** > **Metadata** > **Sensitive species list** lists the sensitive species you have photos of and says why each is there. From a species' **⋯** menu, choose **Mark as not sensitive**; it moves to **Marked not sensitive**, where **Treat as sensitive again** restores it. Click **Add species** to protect any other species.

This only affects what other people see. Your own library files, the app, API keys and integrations such as Immich and iNaturalist (which obscures sensitive species itself) always keep exact locations, and downloading your own originals gives you the file as it is.

### Stopping a link

A link with an expiry stops working on its own. Until then, the list shows when it expires. After that, it's marked **Expired**.

To stop a link sooner, or one set to **Never**, click **Revoke** next to it and confirm. It stops working immediately and for good: anyone with the link loses access.

Revoked and expired links stay in the list, dimmed, so you can see what you shared. Create a new link any time.
