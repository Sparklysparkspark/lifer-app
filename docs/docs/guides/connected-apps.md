# Connected apps

A connected app is a separate program that works with your library through Lifer, such as a culling app or the Lightroom plugin. You see and manage them all in **Settings > Integrations > Connected apps**.

## Allowing an app

An app asks before it connects. Lifer shows its name and what it will be able to do, and you choose **Allow** or **Don't Allow**. An app that opened on its own also shows a 6-digit code: check that Lifer shows the same one.

When you install a suggested app from its page in Lifer, the page lists what the app will be able to do next to the button. Pressing it is your answer, so the app connects without asking again the first time you open it from Lifer. That allowance lasts 30 minutes, covers only what the page listed, and works once. Until the app connects it shows in Connected apps, where **Cancel** withdraws it.

## What an app may do

Each app keeps only what you allowed. Changes an app makes to your photos and collection are listed in Connected apps, newest first, and you can undo each one. **Revoke** disconnects an app; it asks again the next time it wants to connect.

## Opening an app inside Lifer

Some apps can show inside Lifer's window as well as in their own. Such an app may ask "Open inside Lifer from now on?". Nothing changes unless you choose **Yes**, and you can switch **Open inside Lifer** off for that app in Connected apps at any time.
