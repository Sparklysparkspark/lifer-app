# Filters and pinned filters

The **Filters** button in the Gallery, on albums and on trips narrows which photos show. The [Gallery guide](./gallery-and-search.md#filters) lists every filter. This page covers combining them and pinning the ones you use often.

## Several values in one filter

Many filters take more than one value. Photos matching **any** of them show:

- **Region**: search for a region and pick it, then pick another. Each one shows as a chip; click its **×** to remove it. Tick **No region set yet** to include photos without one.
- **Place name** and **Tag**: pick one, then pick another the same way.
- **Rarity**: click each tier you want.
- **Shot type**: pick any number in each group.
- **Rating**: a range, like at least 3 stars **Up to** 5.

Different filters narrow each other. **Region** British Columbia and Alberta, with **Rating** 3 to 5 stars, shows 3 to 5 star photos from either province.

Your filters are part of the page address, so the back button and a bookmark bring them back.

The same filters build a [smart album](./albums-and-sharing.md#smart-albums): set them in the Gallery and choose **⋯ > Save as smart album**.

## Pinned filters {#pins}

Pin a set of filters you use often, like "BC raptors, 4 stars and up", and it's one click away.

1. Set the filters (and a search, if you like) in the Gallery.
2. Click **⋯ > Pin these filters**.
3. Name it. Lifer suggests a name from the filters. Click **Save**.

Pinned filters show as chips under the Gallery title. Click one to apply it; the chip is highlighted while its filters are on. The first five show as chips, and the rest are in the **⋯** next to them.

To rename, reorder or remove pins, click **⋯ > Manage pinned filters**:

- Type a new name in the box. It saves when you click away.
- Use the arrows to move a pin up or down.
- **Save as smart album** turns the pin into a smart album and opens it.
- **Unpin** removes it. The photos and filters are not affected.

You can pin up to 50 filter sets, with names up to 80 characters. Pins are saved with your account, not just on this device.

If a pinned filter names a tag, region or album that's since been deleted, that part simply matches nothing.
