# Stats

The **Stats** page, in the top bar, turns your archive into numbers and charts.

![The Stats page](/img/stats.jpg)

## What counts

Stats are built from your **keepers**: photos you've edited and kept (with an edited JPEG), plus photos you only have as a RAW, except any you rated 1 star. Rate an ID-only shot 1 star to keep it out of your stats.

Click **Photos** at the top to choose what counts: **All keepers**, **Featured only** (species cover photos) or **Top rated (5-star)**, and whether to **Include RAW photos**. The panel shows how many photos your choices count. They apply to every photo count and species count on the page except the sections marked as covering your whole library, and Lifer remembers the RAW choice.

## What's on the page

- At the top, your life list: **Total photographed**, how many species at each rarity (**By rarity**), and how many you first photographed each year (**By year**), always across your whole library. **By rarity** counts each species' overall rarity. Pick a region from its menu, like **In Canada**, to count local rarity there instead, among the species you photographed in that region. Click a rarity to open the Gallery on its photos.
- **Photos over time:** how many photos you took each month, each bar split by animal group: birds, mammals, reptiles and amphibians, fish and sea life, and other. The legend gives each group's count. **Years** shows one bar per year instead. Rest the pointer on a bar for its counts. Photos without a date can't be placed on it; the line under the chart says how many there are.
  - The chart always spans at least a year (five years in **Years**), so a few months of photos sit on a real calendar with the empty months marked.
  - Drag the handles of the strip under the chart to pick a range of dates, drag the green window to move it, or drag across the strip to draw a new one. From the keyboard, focus a handle and press the arrow keys, or Home and End to jump to the ends. The chart zooms in on the range, with the months around it faded for context, and everything below it counts only photos from those dates: the counts, gear, settings, time of day and countries. **Your collection** still covers your whole library. Photos without a date are left out while a range is picked.
  - **Show on map** opens the [photo map](./photo-map.md) with just those dates. **All dates** goes back to everything. The range is part of the page address, so Back and reload keep it.
- **At a glance:** **Top camera**, **Favorite focal length**, **Peak shooting time** and **Best month**, plus short insights like your best month for new lifers.
- **Seen again after 25 years**, **Rarely recorded species** and **Species not seen in 25 years** you've photographed, if any. See [badges](./collection-and-checklists.md#badges).
- **By month:** new lifers over time.
- **Your photo distribution:** a scatter chart of any two of focal length, aperture, ISO and shutter speed. Photos taken at the same settings share one dot, which grows with the count. Hover a dot to see its species and thumbnails, then click a thumbnail to open that photo, with arrows through every photo in the dot. **View all** shows them all as a grid. Click **Filters** to narrow the chart by body, lens, year or date range.
- **Gear usage:** photos or species by camera, lens, or camera and lens together.
- **EXIF distribution:** photos by focal length, ISO, aperture or shutter speed, or species by focal length. Click a bar to see all of its photos as a grid, with **Download all**. Click a photo to open it, with arrows through the rest; Esc goes back to the grid.
- **Time of day:** when you take the most photos.
- **Countries photographed in**, with a photo count for each. This uses the region you picked when importing.
- **Your collection**, always across your whole library:
  - **Most photographed** species.
  - **One-and-done species**, with just one photo.
  - **Could use a better photo**: species whose only photo you rated 1 star.
  - **Library health**: photos missing a date or a region, linked to the Gallery so you can fix them. For a region, select the photos taken in one place and choose **Set region…**.
  - **What you shoot**: your top animal groups, your shot types on two scales, and your median focal length, shutter speed and ISO. **By action** splits your photos into **Flying**, **Behaviour** and **At rest**; **By framing** into **Close** and **Environmental**. Each scale adds up to 100% of the photos Lifer could read. Click a group or shot type to open those photos in the Gallery. Shot types need the species-matching model from **Settings > Offline data**.
  - **Year over year**: pick two years to compare species, photos, average focal length and average ISO.

Species names on the page open that species' page.

## Year in review and Tree of life

The buttons at the top of the page open two more views of your library:

- **Year in review**: one year's new species, photos, trips, places, busiest months and gear. Pick another year from the menu. Click a photo under the firsts or **Best photos** to see it full size, use the arrows to move through that section's photos, and press Esc to go back.
- **Tree of life**: see the next section.

## Tree of life

The **Tree of life** groups the species in your downloaded regions by class, order, family and genus, with the ones you've photographed first. Each group shows how many of its species you have, like **12 of 40**, and the species you haven't photographed yet are faded, so you can see what's left to find.

- Click a group's name to open it and see it in the panel on the right (a sheet on a phone). The arrow beside it opens and closes it.
- Click a species to see it in the panel, with **Open species page**. Clicking never closes anything, and the tree stays as you left it when you come back from a species page.
- The panel shows a photo of every species you have in the group, rarest first (scroll it, and **Show 30 more species** adds the next ones), how many of its species you have, your photo count and its **Rarest finds**. Click a group above it in the panel to move up.
- Click any photo to see it full size, with arrows through the photos shown.
- Type in **Jump to a group or species** to open the tree down to it. Press Enter for the next match.
- **Expand one level** opens every group shown one level deeper; **Collapse all** closes them. **Random species**, beside **View**, picks one of your species to look at, from the selected group when one is picked.
- With the keyboard, the arrow keys move, Right opens a group, Left closes it or moves up, Enter shows a row in the panel and Space opens its photo.
- Pick a country above the tree, then an area inside it, to see only that region's species: the counts, the faded species, the panel and **Export CSV** all follow it. Groups with nothing there disappear. **All regions** shows everything again.
- In **Circle**, click a group to zoom into it and the middle to step back out. **Focus on this group** in the panel does the same in the list. Back, the trackpad swipe and the back shortcut step out one group at a time, and Forward steps back in. Each step, region change included, comes back as you left it: the same group selected, the same groups open in the list and the same scroll position. The **Stats** link at the top leaves the page. The segments glide into place as you zoom in or out, change region or change the view; with **Reduce motion** turned on in your system settings they switch at once.
- In **Circle**, the selected segment gets a dark outline; the one you're pointing at gets a lighter one, and the one the keyboard is on also gets a green ring.
- Rest the pointer on a segment, or a row in the list, and the panel on the right shows a **Preview** of it; move off and it goes back to what you selected. Click to select it. Moving between segments with the arrow keys previews each one too. Touch screens don't preview: tap to select.
- In **Circle**, a segment labelled **N more groups** stands in for groups too thin to draw. Click it (or press Enter) to see just those groups across the whole circle; the trail above the circle ends in **N more groups**, and the middle, Back and the trail step out one layer. A very large set pages: the biggest groups first, then another **N more groups**.
- If you pick a region that has none of the group you were looking at, the tree shows the nearest larger group and says so; the species you had selected is dropped when it isn't in that region.
- **Show genera** under **View** adds the genus level. Point at it for what a genus is.

### Rarity in the tree

In **Circle**, the green part of each segment is what you've photographed: a species is all green once you have it, and a group fills outward by its share. The pale part is what you haven't photographed yet.

**Show rarity** under **View** adds a thin band along the outer edge of each segment, in orange: paler for common, deeper for rarer (brighter in dark mode), with the scale in the key above the circle. The green fill stays as it is. A species' band shows its own tier; a group's shows its average, like **Mostly Uncommon, 4 of 20 rare or rarer**, in the middle of the circle and when you point at it. In the list, rows get the same label and colour. With a region picked, it uses that region's local rarity where the region has one (otherwise the species' worldwide rarity). It's on when you pick a region and off without one, until you change it; Lifer remembers your choice.

With a region picked, a species' panel names both when they differ, like **Legendary in Canada** and **Common worldwide**, and **Rarest finds** ranks by the local one.

### Targets and seen in the tree

Species on your targets show the target icon, and species you've seen but not photographed show the eye, as on the species cards. A group says how many it holds, like **3 targets** and **2 seen**, and in **Circle** a small target marks every group holding one (the legend under the circle says so).

- Select a species, then **Add to targets** or **Mark as seen** in the panel (or the reverse to take it off).
- Select a group for **Target 12 species you haven't photographed**, **Mark 12 species as seen** and **Remove 3 targets**. They only change the species shown in the tree: the region you picked, and only your targets while **Show only my targets** is on. Targeting and marking seen skip species you've already photographed.
- Right-click a row or a ring segment for the same actions.
- More than 10 species asks first, with the exact count. A message at the bottom of the screen offers **Undo**.
- **Show only my targets** under **View** narrows the tree and the circle to the groups holding your targets. Lifer remembers it.
- The **3 targets** button beside **View** opens Collection's Targets list for the same region.

## Export to CSV

Click **Export CSV** to save a spreadsheet of your keepers (matching the choices at the top and any dates picked on **Photos over time**) with species, date, camera, lens, settings, rating and country. The desktop app asks where to save it. In a browser it downloads.
