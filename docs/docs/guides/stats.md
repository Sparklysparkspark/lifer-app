# Stats

The **Stats** page, in the top bar, turns your archive into numbers and charts.

![The Stats page](/img/stats.jpg)

## What counts

Stats are built from your **keepers**: photos you've edited and kept (with an edited JPEG), except any you rated 1 star. RAW-only imports don't count. Rate an ID-only shot 1 star to keep it out of your stats.

Use the menu at the top to show **All keepers**, **Featured only** (species cover photos), or **Top rated (5-star)**.

## What's on the page

- **At a glance:** **Top camera**, **Favorite focal length**, **Peak shooting time** and **Best month**, plus short insights like your best month for new lifers.
- **Rediscovered**, **Ghost species** and **Lost species** you've photographed, if any. See [badges](./collection-and-checklists.md#badges).
- **By month:** new lifers or total keepers over time.
- **Your photo distribution:** a scatter chart of any two of focal length, aperture, ISO and shutter speed. Click a dot to open the photo.
- **Gear usage:** photos or species by camera, lens, or camera and lens together.
- **EXIF distribution:** photos by focal length, ISO, aperture or shutter speed, or species by focal length. Click a bar to see those photos, with **Download all**.
- **Time of day:** when you take the most photos.
- **Countries photographed in**, with a photo count for each. This uses the region you picked when importing.
- **Collection intelligence**, always across your whole library:
  - **Most photographed** species.
  - **One-and-done species**, with just one photo.
  - **Could use a better photo**: species whose only photo you rated 1 star.
  - **Archive health**: photos missing a date, linked to the Gallery so you can fix them.
  - **Photography DNA**: your top animal groups, your mix of kinds of shot (portrait, flight, behavior, environmental), and your median focal length, shutter speed and ISO. The kinds of shot need the species-matching model from **Settings > Offline data**.
  - **Year over year**: pick two years to compare species, photos, average focal length and average ISO.
- **Collection breakdown:** total species collected, how many in each tier, and how many you first photographed each year.

## Export to CSV

Click **Export CSV** to save a spreadsheet of your keepers (matching the menu at the top) with species, date, camera, lens, settings, rating and country. The desktop app asks where to save it. In a browser it downloads.
