# Phone companion app: what photographers ask for

Web research, October 2026. Question: what do wildlife, bird and nature photographers (and people with large photo archives) ask for when they want their archive or life list on their phone?

**Coverage note.** Reddit, BirdForum, Fred Miranda, DPReview and BCG Forums all blocked direct page reads (HTTP 403 or fetch refused), so quotes from those sites appear only where a search result surfaced the wording, and are marked "(search snippet)". The deepest evidence comes from the iNaturalist Community Forum, Immich GitHub discussions, the Adobe Community and Lightroom Queen forums, where full threads could be read. Frequency ratings below reflect how often a request turned up across separate threads and how many votes or replies it carried, not a formal count.

## 1. Top 15 requests, ranked by frequency

| # | Request | Frequency | Audience |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Offline access to my own photos and albums while travelling or out of signal | Common | All, strongest with self-hosters and naturalists |
| 2 | Quick "have I seen / photographed this species before?" lookup on the phone | Common | Birders, naturalists |
| 3 | Life list that shows my own photos, not stock photos | Common | Birders, naturalists |
| 4 | Show my best shots to someone in person, quickly and reliably | Common | All |
| 5 | Search the whole archive by species or keyword from the phone | Common | General wildlife, pro |
| 6 | Species near me that I have not seen or photographed yet (targets) | Common | Birders, naturalists |
| 7 | Fast browsing of the whole library on the phone (cached thumbnails) | Occasional to common | Self-hosters |
| 8 | Add a phone, digiscope or back-of-camera shot straight into the right place, without uploading everything | Occasional | Birders, self-hosters |
| 9 | Separate "photographed" list from "seen" list, and "still need a photo" | Occasional | Birders |
| 10 | Compare a new sighting with my past photos of the same or similar species | Occasional | Naturalists |
| 11 | Ratings and keywords made on the phone sync back to the main library | Occasional | Pro, Lightroom users |
| 12 | Control over location data when sharing (sensitive species, home address) | Occasional | Birders, naturalists |
| 13 | Browse my species taxonomically with my own photos, as a personal field guide | Occasional | Naturalists |
| 14 | Keep the phone's storage free (previews on phone, originals at home) | Occasional | All |
| 15 | Rate or pick photos on a phone or tablet while travelling | Occasional (mixed views) | Travel and pro shooters |

## 2. Full list by theme

### Offline and travel

**Offline albums and photos.** People want chosen albums (or the whole library at low resolution) kept on the phone so they work on planes, abroad and in the field. Common. Audience: self-hosters, naturalists, travellers.
- Immich #7627 (68 upvotes on the request): "keep certain albums available locally on my device while traveling" ([source](https://github.com/immich-app/immich/discussions/7627))
- iNaturalist forum: "I frequently use the mobile app as my personal herbaria... often don't have phone service to access the web interface." ([source](https://forum.inaturalist.org/t/allow-offline-access-to-observation-data-and-map/12840))
- iNaturalist forum (2026): "the ability to save observations to reference while off the grid is extremely powerful." ([source](https://forum.inaturalist.org/t/allow-offline-access-to-observation-data-and-map/12840))

**Whole-library thumbnail cache.** Browse everything quickly, even on a slow link to the home server. Occasional to common. Audience: self-hosters.
- Immich #9706: "Option to cache low resolution thumbnails and even original assets on device" ([source](https://github.com/immich-app/immich/discussions/9706))
- Same thread: "Right now, I mostly just avoid using Immich altogether when I am away from home." ([source](https://github.com/immich-app/immich/discussions/9706))
- Same thread: "Thumbnails are small enough that the entire library fits on a phone." ([source](https://github.com/immich-app/immich/discussions/9706))

**Sync that survives bad connections.** Occasional. Audience: travellers, Lightroom users.
- Lightroom Queen: "it just doesn't work when I'm traveling or on a cruise ship." ([source](https://www.lightroomqueen.com/community/threads/ideal-or-least-bad-workflow-while-on-the-go.40608/))

### Life list with my photos

**Life list illustrated with my own photos.** Common. Audience: birders, naturalists.
- iNaturalist forum: "It's really frustrating when I switch to the Species view that my own photos get replaced by the default ones." ([source](https://forum.inaturalist.org/t/show-my-own-photos-in-species-list-in-new-life-list/20692))
- iNaturalist forum: "I would really like to enable to see all the species I have observed in taxonomic order with my own photos." ([source](https://forum.inaturalist.org/t/is-there-a-way-to-create-a-nice-lifelist-with-my-own-photos-not-default-taxon-photos/35472))
- Related: users want to choose which of their photos represents a species, and iNat does not allow picking one ([source](https://forum.inaturalist.org/t/select-a-specific-photo-in-lists/5158)).

**A "photographed" list kept apart from "seen".** Occasional. Audience: birders.
- Birda defines its photo life list as "a list of all the species that you have logged on Birda where the sighting includes a photo" ([source](https://support.birda.org/en/articles/8036845-what-is-a-photo-life-list))
- A paid Notion life-list template sells tracking of birds "seen and/or photographed", with a "still need a photo" box, usable "via your mobile phone" in the field (search snippet) ([source](https://marktittley.gumroad.com/l/Lifelist))
- A BCG Forums birder says their life list is only birds they have seen and photographed (search snippet) ([source](https://bcgforums.com/threads/e-bird-or-other-app.51205/))

**Personal field guide from my own photos.** Taxonomically sorted, offline, my photos only. Occasional. Audience: naturalists.
- iNaturalist forum: "I am looking for downloading all my inat pictures from observations and creating a taxonomically ordered PDF" ([source](https://forum.inaturalist.org/t/create-offline-lifelist-of-your-observations/28740))

### In the field

**"Have I seen or photographed this before?"** A fast species lookup against my own records. Common. Audience: birders, naturalists.
- iNaturalist staff, on the new in-app search: "Or just to easily see if I've observed something before." ([source](https://forum.inaturalist.org/t/search-sort-through-your-own-observations-in-the-iphone-app/84457))
- iNaturalist forum: "I find the blue checkmark badges that eBird / Merlin display for species I've observed very useful" ([source](https://forum.inaturalist.org/t/checkmarks-for-species-on-life-list/9400))
- Same thread: "There would not be a single iNat session where I am not clicking on the taxa" ([source](https://forum.inaturalist.org/t/checkmarks-for-species-on-life-list/9400))

**Targets: what near me have I not seen yet.** Common. Audience: birders, naturalists.
- iNaturalist forum (2024): "A way to view species I have yet to observe in the nearby area would be awesome." ([source](https://forum.inaturalist.org/t/find-nearby-unobserved-species-in-inat-next/55802))
- iNaturalist forum (2019): "show me the most commonly observed life that I haven't observed myself yet" ([source](https://forum.inaturalist.org/t/show-me-what-i-havent-seen-yet/2267))
- Same thread: "Yes, a pre-fab filter choice would be nice." ([source](https://forum.inaturalist.org/t/show-me-what-i-havent-seen-yet/2267))

**Compare a new find with my past photos.** Occasional. Audience: naturalists.
- iNaturalist forum: "Often I find a moth that I know I've seen before, but it may have been a year or two ago" ([source](https://forum.inaturalist.org/t/add-a-compare-function-that-compares-a-users-own-observations/17089))
- Side-by-side compare is also requested for the new iOS app ([source](https://forum.inaturalist.org/t/bring-the-the-side-by-side-compare-with-observations-feature-to-the-new-ios-app-to-improve-identification-accuracy/83798))

**Revisit places and individuals over time.** Rare. Audience: naturalists.
- iNaturalist forum: "I like to go back to an area and see how different individual organisms change during the year." ([source](https://forum.inaturalist.org/t/allow-offline-access-to-observation-data-and-map/12840))
- Related request: show my past observation pins on the map ([source](https://forum.inaturalist.org/t/show-my-past-observation-pins-when-marking-new-observations-on-map/9503/16))

**Getting a camera or digiscope shot onto the phone for a quick ID.** Occasional. Audience: birders.
- iNaturalist forum: people photograph the camera's screen with the phone to "get an initial ID of something unique to me" ([source](https://forum.inaturalist.org/t/photo-of-screens-back-of-camera/32755?page=2))
- Same thread: "A back of the camera shot is good enough for an ID" ([source](https://forum.inaturalist.org/t/photo-of-screens-back-of-camera/32755?page=2))

### Showing and sharing

**Show my favourite shots in person, without lag.** Common. Audience: all.
- Immich #7627: "if a server is down or on a slow network request, it can ruin the experience." ([source](https://github.com/immich-app/immich/discussions/7627))
- Immich #7627: "I'd love to show them some photos of my dog. Hard to do at 35,000 feet!" ([source](https://github.com/immich-app/immich/discussions/7627))
- A DPReview member wanted an app "to create an offline gallery of top photos to share with friends" and was not interested in editing (search snippet) ([source](https://www.dpreview.com/forums/threads/ios-app-for-viewing-offline-photos.4827172/))

**Share a daily selection while on a trip.** Rare to occasional. Audience: travellers.
- Lightroom Queen: a traveller wants to pick, edit and share a 30 to 40 photo selection every day ([source](https://www.lightroomqueen.com/community/threads/ideal-or-least-bad-workflow-while-on-the-go.40608/))
- Same thread: "I have to wait until all 1,000 images upload before I can set the album to shared." ([source](https://www.lightroomqueen.com/community/threads/ideal-or-least-bad-workflow-while-on-the-go.40608/))

**Location privacy when sharing.** Occasional. Audience: birders, naturalists.
- iNaturalist forum asks for a "hide EXIF geolocation" option because photos taken at home can expose an address; the current workaround is to "manually strip the EXIF geolocation data" ([source](https://forum.inaturalist.org/t/introduce-new-geoprivacy-option-hide-exif-geolocation/78548))
- Tweeters list (2025) on sensitive species: photos of Gyrfalcons "will appear... in the 'Recent Photos' for a particular region, along with the photographers name." ([source](https://mailman11.u.washington.edu/pipermail/tweeters/2025-February/006749.html))
- eBird hides 325 sensitive taxa, including owls at roosts, showing the risk is real ([source](https://www.audubon.org/magazine/ebird-now-censoring-sightings-325-risk-species))

### Search and organisation

**Search the full archive by species or keyword from the phone.** Common. Audience: general wildlife, pro.
- Adobe feature request (46 votes, 410 replies, about 17,800 views): "In Lightroom Mobile I'm frustrated because I cannot search for photos tagged with keywords from Classic." ([source](https://community.adobe.com/feature-requests-676/p-ability-to-sync-lightroom-classic-keywords-with-the-lightroom-ecosystem-666425/index12.html))
- Lightroom Queen: "I am looking for a workflow where I have access to whole my Lightroom catalog even if I'm away from my computer." ([source](https://www.lightroomqueen.com/community/threads/workflow-for-have-access-to-the-whole-lightroom-catalog-from-the-phone.40737/))
- iNaturalist forum: "I'd love a feature where I can search my own observations for all records that match a particular taxa" ([source](https://forum.inaturalist.org/t/allow-offline-access-to-observation-data-and-map/12840))

**Keep the library's structure on the phone.** Albums, folders and smart collections, not a flat dump. Occasional. Audience: Lightroom users.
- Lightroom Queen: "Published collection is a flat structure which loses all folders hierarchy, collections and smart collections." ([source](https://www.lightroomqueen.com/community/threads/workflow-for-have-access-to-the-whole-lightroom-catalog-from-the-phone.40737/))

### Getting phone photos in

**Add a phone photo straight to an album, without backing up the whole camera roll.** Occasional. Audience: self-hosters.
- Immich #12190 (38 upvotes): "I rather like to backup only those photos which I personally care about." ([source](https://github.com/immich-app/immich/discussions/12190))
- Same thread: "It is a big hassle to first upload the media to the general timeline" ([source](https://github.com/immich-app/immich/discussions/12190))

### Editing metadata on the phone

**Ratings, flags and keywords made on the phone flow back.** Occasional. Audience: pro, Lightroom users.
- Adobe: "Keywords do not sync correctly: When added on the mobile app (iOS), they do not appear in LR classic" ([source](https://community.adobe.com/feature-requests-676/p-ability-to-sync-lightroom-classic-keywords-with-the-lightroom-ecosystem-666425/index12.html))
- Adobe: "It cannot be a solution to do the sync of the keywords manually." ([source](https://community.adobe.com/feature-requests-676/p-ability-to-sync-lightroom-classic-keywords-with-the-lightroom-ecosystem-666425/index12.html))
- Ratings reported syncing one way only in some versions ([source](https://community.adobe.com/questions-675/changes-sync-from-lightroom-classic-to-mobile-but-not-mobile-to-classic-936438))

**Rating or picking on a phone or tablet.** Occasional, and mixed. Mostly travel shooters on an iPad, often as a stopgap until they reach a computer. Little evidence of wildlife photographers wanting to do serious picking on a phone screen.
- Lightroom Queen: "no better solution than importing in LrM 2-3k photos every day" ([source](https://www.lightroomqueen.com/community/threads/ideal-or-least-bad-workflow-while-on-the-go.40608/))
- An SLR Lounge review found the iPad app useful for picking while travelling but said the editing side "simply falls short" for pro work (search snippet) ([source](https://www.slrlounge.com/lightroom-cc-mobile-for-ipad-review-can-it-fit-into-a-pro-workflow/))

### Storage

**Previews on the phone, originals at home.** Occasional. Audience: all.
- A Mylio review describes a 45,000-photo library using about 40 to 50 GB on an iPhone, with full-size files fetched from home over Wi-Fi when needed (search snippet) ([source](https://digital-photography-school.com/mylio-photos-review/))
- Immich #7627 proposes "a slightly lower quality variant" kept like offline music playlists, apart from the phone gallery ([source](https://github.com/immich-app/immich/discussions/7627))
- A BirdForum user found the Audubon app, which stores photos with sightings, took a lot of phone storage (search snippet) ([source](https://www.birdforum.net/threads/birding-apps.409994/))

## 3. Complaints about existing apps

- **Lightroom mobile and Classic.** Keywords do not sync between Classic and mobile, so the phone cannot search a keyworded archive ([source](https://community.adobe.com/feature-requests-676/p-ability-to-sync-lightroom-classic-keywords-with-the-lightroom-ecosystem-666425/index12.html)). "The missing link is between Adobe Cloud and Classic." ([source](https://www.lightroomqueen.com/community/threads/workflow-for-have-access-to-the-whole-lightroom-catalog-from-the-phone.40737/)). Only selected collections sync, and stalls and stale previews are reported ([source](https://community.adobe.com/t5/lightroom-classic-discussions/lightroom-classic-will-not-sync-to-mobile-shows-sync-in-process-with-no-progress/m-p/11323042/highlight/true)). Cloud storage caps: "I couldn't find any option to upgrade to a bigger storage." ([source](https://community.adobe.com/questions-680/lightroom-mobile-more-cloud-storage-1617077))
- **Immich mobile.** Slow when the server is far away or down: "In general I find the application much less responsive then the web view." ([source](https://github.com/immich-app/immich/discussions/9706)). "it often takes very long for the app to open" when the server is unreachable ([source](https://github.com/immich-app/immich/discussions/7627)). Cached previews get cleared ([source](https://github.com/immich-app/immich/discussions/9706)). Uploading to an album forces a timeline backup first ([source](https://github.com/immich-app/immich/discussions/12190)).
- **iNaturalist.** Life list swaps your photos for stock ones; staff say site load blocks the fix ([source](https://forum.inaturalist.org/t/show-my-own-photos-in-species-list-in-new-life-list/20692)). No built-in "not yet seen" filter in the app ([source](https://forum.inaturalist.org/t/find-nearby-unobserved-species-in-inat-next/55802)). Offline use is weak: on poor signal "the syncing is wonky" (search snippet) ([source](https://forum.inaturalist.org/t/inaturalist-next-with-poor-no-cell-phone-connectivity/55541)). EXIF location can leak a home address ([source](https://forum.inaturalist.org/t/introduce-new-geoprivacy-option-hide-exif-geolocation/78548)).
- **eBird and Macaulay Library.** Photos must match a checklist's date, time and place, so you cannot drop a photo from another outing onto a checklist; limit of 20 photos per species per checklist ([source](https://support.ebird.org/en/support/solutions/articles/48000825713-uploading-media)). Hidden checklists can still surface photos in regional "Recent Photos" ([source](https://mailman11.u.washington.edu/pipermail/tweeters/2025-February/006749.html)).
- **Google Photos.** "High quality" mode compresses to 16 MP and RAW files are converted or count against paid storage (search snippet) ([source](https://www.techradar.com/news/google-photos-warns-against-using-its-compressed-high-quality-mode-heres-why)).
- **Mylio.** Phone and remote access need the Mylio Photos+ subscription; after cancelling, "syncing and protection features will not be available" ([source](https://support.mylio.com/en/articles/8104021-what-happens-to-my-files-if-i-decide-to-cancel-my-mylio-photos-subscription)).
- **Photo Mechanic.** No phone or tablet version exists ([source](https://docs.camerabits.com/support/solutions/articles/48000361279-is-there-a-tablet-or-phone-version-of-photo-mechanic-)).
- **PhotoPrism.** No official mobile app; the community Android client is online only ([source](https://f-droid.org/packages/ua.com.radiokot.photoprism/)).

## 4. Things people say they do not want

- **Subscriptions and cloud storage fees.** Adobe users call $19.99 a month for 1 TB expensive when they only want storage (search snippet) ([source](https://community.adobe.com/t5/lightroom-ecosystem-cloud-based-discussions/confused-about-storage-quota-for-photographer-plans/td-p/9430793)). Mylio's phone access is subscription only ([source](https://support.mylio.com/en/articles/8104024-why-is-mylio-photos-only-available-as-a-subscription-and-not-as-a-one-time-purchase)). One Lightroom user was testing a self-hosted NAS setup instead ([source](https://www.lightroomqueen.com/community/threads/workflow-for-have-access-to-the-whole-lightroom-catalog-from-the-phone.40737/)).
- **Forced upload of everything.** "I rather like to backup only those photos which I personally care about." ([source](https://github.com/immich-app/immich/discussions/12190)). Offline albums are proposed as separate from backup, kept out of the phone's own gallery ([source](https://github.com/immich-app/immich/discussions/7627)).
- **Gamification, badges and competition.** iNaturalist users: "I don't like the idea of competitiveness between participants." and "gaming the system for vanity numbers (= badges) is not a happy or meaningful choice." ([source](https://forum.inaturalist.org/t/create-badges-for-user-profiles/4209)). Staff closed the request: "we won't be adding gamification elements to iNat." ([source](https://forum.inaturalist.org/t/create-badges-for-user-profiles/4209)). Some users like light scorekeeping, so opinion is not unanimous ([source](https://forum.inaturalist.org/t/how-much-do-you-gamify-inaturalist/55549/2)).
- **Leaking locations.** People do not want EXIF GPS or sensitive-species sites exposed when they share ([source](https://forum.inaturalist.org/t/introduce-new-geoprivacy-option-hide-exif-geolocation/78548), [source](https://mailman11.u.washington.edu/pipermail/tweeters/2025-February/006749.html)).
- **An app that depends on the server being reachable.** "Right now, I mostly just avoid using Immich altogether when I am away from home." ([source](https://github.com/immich-app/immich/discussions/9706))
- **Heavy editing on the phone.** The DPReview offline-gallery poster was not interested in editing features (search snippet) ([source](https://www.dpreview.com/forums/threads/ios-app-for-viewing-offline-photos.4827172/)), and reviewers say serious editing waits for a computer ([source](https://www.slrlounge.com/lightroom-cc-mobile-for-ipad-review-can-it-fit-into-a-pro-workflow/)).
