# Viewer consistency

Every full-size photo in the signed-in app opens in the one shared viewer (`components/Lightbox.tsx`) with the same corner controls (Full screen, the **⋯** photo menu where a menu applies, Close) and the same keys (Esc closes, F full screen, arrows step, 1 to 5 rate). This page is the audit of where photos open and what each surface shows.

## How a surface gets the menu

- **A page with its own tile menu** passes it as `slide.menu` (Gallery, species page, Versions dialog), so the viewer offers exactly what the grid does.
- **Any other library photo** passes `slide.photo` and its handlers, usually through `librarySlide()` (`lib/librarySlide.ts`). The viewer then builds the standard `PhotoMenu`: rate, View species, Correct the ID (`onReassign`), Add to album, Location (`coordinates.onChange`), Adjust date and time (`onChanged`), Similar photos, Versions, Download, Show in Finder, Copy path, Open in editor, Send to iNaturalist, Delete (`onDeleted`, instant with Undo). An item whose handler or data is missing is left out.
- **Photos that aren't in the library yet, or aren't yours** get no menu, or only the actions that apply (import review: Compare and Remove from this import; a shared page: Download when sharing allows it).

The map's photo lists page through `/gallery` (`components/map/MapPhotoViewer.tsx`): the viewer loads the next 60 a few photos before the end, and the step past the last loaded photo waits for them instead of wrapping, so a 5,000-photo cluster never loads all at once.

## Time taken

`formatTakenAt()` (`lib/format.ts`) shows "Nov 17, 2026, 7:42 PM" in the locale's 12 or 24 hour clock. The camera's clock (stored as if UTC when the file had no zone) is shown as shot, never moved to the viewer's zone; a zoned time shows in the viewer's zone and its tooltip adds the offset. Seconds are in the tooltip. A typed day (noon UTC) shows the date only. Lists that don't send `takenAtZoned` show the date only, as before, since their time can't be placed. Gallery items, trip photos, species captures and version link candidates now send it.

## Controls

- Corner buttons and the version pill sit on a dark see-through chip with a light rim (`bg-black/55`, `ring-white/25`), so they read on a white photo and on black. The arrows use PhotoArrows' photo tone for the same reason.
- 44px to touch below the `sm` breakpoint, 32px above; the photo starts below them.
- In full screen (and on a playing video) the controls fade after a second without the mouse and come back on any move or Tab.
- The buttons sit top right, clear of the macOS traffic lights; the viewer keeps its own title bar drag strip.
